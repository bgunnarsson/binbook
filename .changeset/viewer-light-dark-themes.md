The viewer has a light and a dark theme. Light (Cloud: cool white, slate text,
indigo accent) and dark (Geist: near-black, `#ededed` text, a white primary)
follow the OS by default; the sun/moon button in the toolbar pins one, and the
choice is remembered. Alongside it the chrome is restyled: Poppins for the UI
with JetBrains Mono kept for code, borderless inputs, segmented controls for
Canvas/Docs and the viewport sizes, a soft highlight for the selected story,
and count pills on the panel tabs.

The canvas iframe now shows a project's configured `background` in the dark
theme too; before, the browser painted a white backdrop over it.
